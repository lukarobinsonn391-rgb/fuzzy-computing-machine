<h2><strong>What "Free" LLC Formation Actually Costs</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Every service on this list advertises a </span><span style="font-weight: 400;">0ornear-</span><span style="font-weight: 400;">0 entry price, and that's technically accurate: the formation filing fee itself is often free.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">But a genuinely $0 LLC almost never stays that way past year one. Watch for these costs before you compare headline prices:</span></p>
<ul>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>State filing fees.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Every service charges these separately, and they range from under $50 to several hundred dollars depending on your state.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Registered agent renewal.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Most states require one, and most services offer year one free or cheap, then charge $125 to $249 annually starting in year two.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>EIN filing.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Often included free (the IRS doesn't charge for this directly), but some services charge a service fee to file it on your behalf.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Operating agreement.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Sometimes bundled, sometimes a $35 to $50 add-on.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Compliance and annual report filing.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Commonly priced around $99 to $199 a year as an ongoing service.</span></li>
<li style="font-weight: 400;"><strong>Rush processing.</strong><span style="font-weight: 400;"> Standard on premium tiers, an add-on (commonly $99) on lower tiers.</span></li>
</ul>

<h2><strong>Frequently Asked Questions</strong></h2>
<h3><strong>Is $0 LLC formation really free?&nbsp;</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The formation filing itself is often free, but you'll still pay your state's filing fee, and most services charge for registered agent service starting in year two, along with optional add-ons like an operating agreement or compliance filing.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Which LLC service is cheapest overall?&nbsp;</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It depends on your state's filing fee and which add-ons you need. Bizee's free first-year registered agent and ZenBusiness's $0 Starter plan are consistently among the lowest total year-one costs across 2026 comparisons.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Do I need a registered agent?&nbsp;</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Most states require one. Some services include it free for the first year, while others charge for it from day one, so factor this into your total cost comparison rather than looking at the formation price alone.</span></p>
<h3><strong>How fast can I actually get my LLC formed?&nbsp;</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Standard processing across most services runs roughly 5 to 10 business days, with expedited or rush options (often for an extra fee) bringing that down to as little as one business day. Your state's own processing time is added on top of the service provider's turnaround.</span></p>
<h3><strong>Should I just form my LLC myself instead of using a service?&nbsp;</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">You can, and the only required cost is your state filing fee. A formation service mainly saves you time and reduces the risk of a paperwork mistake, which matters more in states with complex or unusual filing requirements.</span></p>
<h2><strong>The Bottom Line</strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">There's no single best LLC service for everyone, and the "$0 formation" headline on most of these providers' homepages tells you almost nothing about what you'll actually pay by year two.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Compare registered agent renewal costs, what's genuinely bundled versus sold as an add-on, and how each provider's pricing lines up with what your specific state requires.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Northwest and ZenBusiness lead most 2026 rankings for overall value, LegalZoom and Rocket Lawyer make sense if you want legal support built in, and Swyft Filings or Bizee are worth a look if you'd rather build your own package than buy a fixed bundle.</span></p>
